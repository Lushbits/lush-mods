# Lush Mods

Lush's addons for WoW: Forever, and a small app that installs them and keeps them up to date.

## Get the app

1. Download **[LushUpdater.exe](https://github.com/Lushbits/lush-mods/releases/latest/download/LushUpdater.exe)** and put it somewhere it can stay (not Downloads).
2. Run it. Windows will warn that it's from an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Switch on the addons you want. They install straight away and update on their own from then on.
4. Tick **Start with Windows** so it keeps running in the tray.

Closing the window keeps the app running in the tray, next to the clock. Right-click the green **L** to quit.

Switching an addon off removes it. Your addon settings are kept either way.

If the app can't find your game, click **Change** and pick your World of Warcraft folder.

## What's in this repo

- `index.json` is the list of addons the app reads.
- [Releases](https://github.com/Lushbits/lush-mods/releases) hold a zip for each addon version, and the app itself.

You can also install an addon by hand: download its zip from Releases and unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`.
